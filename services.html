Our Services

Regyana Bed & Breakfast offers comfortable accommodation and additional services to make your stay and special occasions enjoyable.

Accommodation

Regyana Bed & Breakfast provides comfortable accommodation for guests looking for a relaxing place to stay.

Accommodation starts at approximately R600 per night.

Breakfast

Guests can also enjoy breakfast during their stay. Breakfast is available at an additional cost of approximately R70 per person.
